Summary
Based on the ECP M730 platform, this thesis mainly analyzes the dynamic model,
Fault Detection and Isolation (FDI), and Fault Tolerance Control (FTC) of the ma-
glev system. In this thesis, Single-Input Single-Output system (SISO) and Multiple-
Input Multiple-Output system (MISO) are the two main analysis scenarios. Time-
invariant step signals are the main types of faults. In order to diagnose such faults in
time, this article applies the observer-based method to obtain the residuals. Generalized
Likelihood Ratio Test (GLRT) is performed on these residuals to complete the fault
diagnosis and isolation. For fault-tolerant control, this thesis adopts two methods,
fault accommodation and fault reconfiguration, to restore the SISO and MISO sys-
tem’s failure respectively.

1.1
Introduction
1.1.1
The magnetic levitation system
The use of magnetic levitation systems in the industry is increasing. Because the
lack of physical contact, the magnetic levitation system provides less resistance, less
noise, lower cost, but higher speed. Today, the two most common application areas
for magnetic levitation are magnetic levitation trains [ZLL19] and magnetic levita-
tion bearings[Wan+20]. Maglev train is through the electromagnetic force to levitate
the vehicle upon the track contactless and run the train by the linear motor. Mag-
netic bearing uses magnetic force to suspend the rotor in the air so that there is no
mechanical contact between the rotor and the stator. In these magnetic levitation
systems, the stability and fault tolerance of the system are very important since they
are the prerequisites of safe running. Through the research of the ECP M730, the
teaching experiment equipment of magnetic levitation system, this thesis can lay a
good foundation for future research on magnetic levitation trains and bearings.

1.1.2
Fault Detection and Isolation (FDI)
Faults in the industrial system may cause degraded performance of the control system,
decrease product performance and damage to the system or personnel. To avoid these
undesirable consequences, fault detection should be realized as soon as possible by
identifying the initial impact related to the fault.
After the fault is isolated, the
control system must be reconfigured, and it is best to make minimal changes to the
system structure to reduce performance degradation.
1.1.3
Fault-Tolerant Control (FTC)
The fault-tolerant control (FTC) system has the functions to maintain the stability
and performance of the system acceptably under a series of failure conditions. Gen-
erally, FTC systems combine fault detection and identification (FDI) with controller
reconfiguration (CR) in an overall control system to to maintain closed-loop stability
and good performance levels under a set of predefined faults.
1.2
Literature review
All the literature and the materials are divided into three categories. They are the
application of the magnetic levitation, the way of the fault detection and isolation
and the fault-tolerant control.
For the application, the book [LLL15] introduces the conception and application of

Maglev technology in detail, especially in Maglev trains which are able to run more
smoothly and quiet compared with wheeled mass transit systems. This technique
has been developed by many countries for recent years. In Japan, superconducting
magnetic leviation (maglev) trains were researched and tested. As an advanced train
by means of a linear synchronous motor (LSM), a superconducting magnet and an
armature coil was proposed [OKO02]. The magnetic bearing has a vital role to play in
the stable and dependable movement of Maglev trains. The book [CJM04] illustrates
the basic knowledge and promising application of modern magnetic bearing technol-
ogy, decreasing the complexity of the magnetic systems so that more cost-eﬀicient
and reliable magnetic bearing components can be gained.
For FDI, there are currently two mainstream methods to achieve FDI. One is based on
parameter estimation and the other is based on state estimation. In the paper[Zha18],
the actuator fault is modelled as a parameter changes and the fault diagnosis is per-
formed through joint state-parameter estimation in the stochastic framework.
In
the paper[Ich+09], a new method to design an observer for the state estimation is
applied for the sensor fault diagnosis. In recent years, with the popularity of deep
learning, fault detection methods based on deep learning have also been widely used
[Lv+16][Jia+18][HH17]. These papers incorporate methods such as classification and
prediction in deep learning into traditional FDI methods to improve the accuracy
of detection. For this thesis, after analyzing the actual situation of ECP M730, the
method of state estimation is applied.
For FTC, there are many FTC methods, such as the fault accommodation[JSC06]
and the fault reconfiguration[EK16]. A new fault estimation module, based on an
adaptive estimator, is first proposed in the paper [JSC06]. There are also deep learn-
ing applications in FTC[Zha+19]. In this thesis, the fault accommodation and the
fault reconfiguration are both applied in the SISO system and MISO system respec-
tively.
Through the reading of the above three aspects of the literature, the methods of
this thesis which are mentioned in the summary are determined.

1.3
The EPC M730
Figure 1.3: EPC M730.
The EPC M730 experimental system is comprised of the three subsystems shown in
the Figure 1.4. The first of these is the electromechanical plant, which consists of
the MagLev apparatus including its actuators and sensors in Figure 1.5. The de-
sign features two high field density rare earth magnets and high flux drive coils to
provide more than 4 cm of controlled levitation range. Laser sensors provide non-
contacting position feedback and incorporate proprietary conditioning electronics for
signal noise reduction and ambient light rejection. The magnet discs can slide along
a plastic vertical rod with reduced friction.
An optional turntable incorporates a
high-speed conductive spin platter that interacts with the permanent in such a way
as to induce a traveling current and cause the magnet to levitate.
The second subsystem is the real-time controller unit which contains the Digital Signal
Processor (DSP) based real-time controller, servo/actuator interfaces and servo am-
plifiers, and auxiliary power supplies. Four 16 bit analog-to-digital converters (ADC)
are used to digitize the laser sensor signal. Two optional auxiliary digital-to-analog
converters (DAC) provide for real-time analog signal measurement.
The third subsystem is the system interface which runs on the simulink under the
Matlab. The interface provides a friendly and powerful experimental environment.

1.4
Problem statement
The aim of the project is to develop and compare different strategies for achieving
fault-tolerance for the ECP M730 MagLev System. It can be resolved through several
project objectives:
• Develop/improve the first-principle mathematical model of M730 MagLev sys-
tem.
• Develop/improve the first-principle mathematical model of M730 MagLev sys-
tem.
• Investigate fault cases and their effects on the performance of the system.
• Develop solutions for fault diagnosis and fault compensation/system reconfigu-
ration.
• Define operation scenarios on which the controllers will be tested.
• Perform simulations of the different operation scenarios.
• Implement the designed schemes on the ECP M730 test setup.
• Define and conduct experiments for the evaluation of the solutions.
• Evaluate the performance of the reconfigured closed-loop system.

The result has a big
error with the real step fault. In order to clarify the cause of this large error, many
experiments have been tried here. Through experiments (The results are shown in
Appendix A), it is found that this error is close to a constant ∆f = 500counts within
a certain fault range.
Therefore, through experimental data, this thesis uses this
constant as compensation and adds back to the estimated fault. In the Figure 5.13, it
shows the result of the estimated fault with compensation. The author speculates that
the reason is that only viscous friction is considered during the modeling process.The
coulomb friction and the gap between the magnet and the glass rod are not considered,
so that the step in the output caused by the step fault is partially offset, thus making
the estimation of the fault is smaller than the actual fault. Besides, when the fault
is smaller than 500counts, the system is not affected by the fault.

CHAPTER 6
Conclusion
The Dynamic Model Of the System
This thesis establishes the dynamic model of the SISO system and the MISO system,
and determines the important parameters in the dynamic system through system
identification. In addition, the operating point is also selected, and the linearization
of the system is completed at the operating point.
Fault Detection and Isolation
A method based the kalman filter is designed to get the residual.
The residuals
got from the kalman filter are met the two main properties. One is that if no fault
happens, the value of the residual should be zero and the other is that if faults happen,
the residual should be sensitive to fault. After the GLR test for the residuals, the
fault can be detected in time.
Fault Tolerance Control
For the SISO system, in order to compensate the faulty system, a linear fault ob-
server is designed. In the simulation, because it is linear, it can accurately estimate
the size of the fault when the generated fault value is not large. When the fault value
is very large, there is a big error between the real fault and the estimated fault. In
the experiment, since Coulomb friction and other factors are not considered in the
modeling. There is a constant error between the real fault and the estimated fault.
This constant is obtained based a lot of experiments. In this thesis, a relatively accu-
rate estimate is obtained through compensation. In the compensation of the faulty
system, this constant error is also taken into account. The author thinks that this
part is worth discussing in future work.
For the MISO system, a virtual actuator is designed to implement the fault recon-
figuration. It restores the faulty system by discarding the failed upper actuator and
using lower actuator to hold the magnet to the previous position. In the experiment,
in the process of restoring the faulty system, the constant value ∆f = 500 counts is

CHAPTER 7
Future Work
More accurate model construction
When the model is constructed, viscous is the only friction taken into consideration in
order to simplify the complicated model. The effect of Coulomb friction and the gap
between magnet and glass rod are not considered. Besides, the influence on magnet
force from coil temperature is also ignored in model construction. In future work,
these factors can be taken into account to increase the accuracy of the system model.
More accurate fault observer
The observer designed in this thesis is based on the linear model at operating point.
If the there is a larger fault occurring on actuator, the system output value is far
away from that obtained at operation point, having an negative effect on observer
accuracy. In future work, a more accurate nonlinear observer can be designed to
realize estimation of fault.
More kinds of faults
In this thesis, the main researched fault is the abrupt fault on actuator. In the future
work, the model can be designed to complete fault diagnosis on different type of places
via taking more kinds of faults into consideration, such as sensor fault, plant fault,
incipient fault and intermittent fault.
More kinds of systems models
In this thesis, FDI and FTC are realized on two cases that are SISO and MISO
systems. In future, a further discussion and research about FDI and FTC can be
done on SIMO and MIMO systems.
